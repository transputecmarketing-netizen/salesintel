How to use it

Five checks. Every month.

Run this checklist once a month with the people who own the spend. Each check takes minutes in the AWS console. The value comes from doing it every time, not from doing it perfectly once.

Bring the owners

Invite the people who can act: an engineer, someone from finance and each budget owner.

Work in order

The checks build on each other. Ownership first makes everything after it faster.

End with actions

Leave with a short list of actions, an owner for each and next month's date.

01

Give every resource an owner

Spend without an owner is spend nobody will question.
Do this
Tag by team, project and environment, then switch on cost allocation tags.
Using
AWS Cost Allocation Tags and Tag Editor.
It is working when
You can group the whole bill by owner, with little left untagged.
Common snag
Tags applied by hand drift. Enforce them with policy where you can.
Owner
Platform team
Bring this to the monthly review, with a date for each action.

02

Switch off what is idle

Pay for what you use, not for what is left running.
Do this
Schedule non-production to stop out of hours, and rightsize from real utilisation.
Using
Instance Scheduler and AWS Compute Optimizer.
It is working when
Non-production is quiet at night and weekends, and sizes match real use.
Common snag
Teams fear breaking things. Start with clearly non-critical systems.
Owner
Engineering leads
Bring this to the monthly review, with a date for each action.

03

Clear out old storage

What nobody deletes, everyone pays for.
Do this
Delete unattached volumes and stale snapshots, and add lifecycle rules to buckets.
Using
EBS and snapshot views, S3 lifecycle policies and S3 Storage Lens.
It is working when
Storage grows with the business, not with time.
Common snag
Uncertainty about what is safe to delete. Agree a retention rule first.
Owner
Infrastructure team
Bring this to the monthly review, with a date for each action.

04

Match commitments to usage

Commit to what is steady. Stay flexible on the rest.
Do this
Check coverage and utilisation before you buy or renew anything.
Using
Savings Plans and Reserved Instance coverage and utilisation reports.
It is working when
Steady workloads are covered and commitments are fully used.
Common snag
Buying on last year's estate. Review after big changes.
Owner
Finance with engineering
Bring this to the monthly review, with a date for each action.

05

Set alerts, then review

Catch surprises between reviews, not on the invoice.
Do this
Put budgets and anomaly alerts in place, and review them with each owner.
Using
AWS Budgets and AWS Cost Anomaly Detection.
It is working when
Owners hear about spikes within days, and act on them.
Common snag
Alerts sent to a shared inbox nobody reads. Route them to owners.
Owner
FinOps lead
Bring this to the monthly review, with a date for each action.

Monthly review

A thirty-minute agenda

Keep it short, keep it regular and always end with owned actions.
01

What changed

Review the month's movement by service and by owner.
02

Anomalies

Walk through any alerts and confirm each was explained or fixed.
03

Last month's actions

Close what is done. Re-date what is not, with a reason.
04

The five checks

Run the checklist and note anything you could not tick.
05

New actions

One owner and one date for each, then book next month.
Remember
Consistency beats depth. A short review every month does more than a deep one every year.
