## Alleged $129m AI impersonation scheme (SDNY) -- ALLEGATIONS ONLY
- Indictment announced Thu 5 Nov 2026 by US Attorney's Office, Southern District of New York (Manhattan federal court). Three defendants (not named in our docs per brief rule). Presumed innocent.
- Alleged period July 2024 to Feb 2026; more than $129m; thousands of companies targeted worldwide. (NBC)
- Method alleged: emails posing as CEO to accounting staff about a "confidential financial transaction"; phone or video calls in which AI mimicked the CEO's voice and sometimes image; staff directed to an impersonated attorney at a major law firm; wire transfers to accounts worldwide; funds converted to crypto. One defendant allegedly mainly impersonated UK attorneys using a handwritten "Lawyer Call" script. (CBS/AP, SecurityWeek/AP)
- Victims listed: NY investment bank, Missouri hospital system, Rhode Island bank, California hospital system, UK fintech, Massachusetts mutual fund company, Florida pet food company. (NBC)
- Seven counts each incl. wire fraud conspiracy, wire fraud, 2x aggravated identity theft, money laundering conspiracy, 2x money laundering. (NBC)
- Arrested June to September (2026): two in Spain, one in the UK; extradition requests pending. (CBS)
- DOJ release uses "SI" (super intelligence) for AI. US Attorney encouraged businesses to consider how best to protect themselves. (NBC)
- DOJ page blocked by bot challenge; relied on NBC, CBS/AP, SecurityWeek/AP.
