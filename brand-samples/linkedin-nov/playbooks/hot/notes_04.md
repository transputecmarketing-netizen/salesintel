## Returns evidence
- McKinsey State of AI 2026 "On the road to ROI" (Aug 2026), 1,719 respondents, fieldwork 4 May-8 Jun 2026. 37% report any EBIT impact (flat). 6% high performers (>=5% EBIT, significant), flat. 80% individual productivity. ~nine in ten use AI; 44% scaling enterprise-wide. 20% say operating costs incl tokens constrain use. 60% expect investment increase. Large firms scaling agents in >=1 function 27% -> 40%; smaller flat 22%. High performers: nearly three quarters redesigned workflows (vs ~quarter others); >2x defined processes for measuring impact; >2x visible senior commitment. https://www.mckinsey.com/~/media/mckinsey/business%20functions/quantumblack/our%20insights/the%20state%20of%20ai/the-state-of-ai-in-2026-on-the-road-to-roi.pdf (PDF not fetchable; figures via Fortune 2 Sep 2026 https://fortune.com/2026/09/02/companies-getting-the-most-from-ai-rethinking-how-work-gets-done-cfo/ and TechTimes 26 Aug 2026 https://www.techtimes.com/articles/325590/20260826/record-ai-spending-cant-move-earnings-needle-94-enterprises-mckinsey-finds.htm). TechTimes: ~90% of vertical use cases still pilot (not used, single source).
- PwC 29th Global CEO Survey, Jan 2026: 4,454 CEOs, 95 countries; 56% no significant financial benefit; 30% revenue up, 26% costs down, 12% both. Fieldwork 30 Sep-10 Nov 2025. Kande: "2026 is shaping up as a decisive year for AI." https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/news-room/press-releases/2026/pwc-2026-global-ceo-survey.html ; WealthBriefing 20 Jan 2026.
- NBER w34836 "Firm Data on AI" (Yotzov, Barrero, Bloom et al), issued Feb 2026, revised Oct 2026: nearly 9,000 execs US, UK, Germany, Australia, Canada, Mexico, South Africa. 78% firms use AI. Productivity +0.2% per year past three years, expect +0.6%/yr next three. Essentially no net employment effect. US spend ~$2,000/employee 2026; 2% of firms >half spend. Execs 2.1 hrs/week. https://www.nber.org/papers/w34836 . Feb version (Register 18 Feb 2026): ~6,000 execs, >80% no discernible impact on employment or productivity. https://www.theregister.com/2026/02/18/ai_productivity_survey/
- NBER w34984 (Baslandze et al, Mar 2026), ~750 executives: positive productivity gains, perceived > measured, lag in revenue realisation. https://nber.org/papers/w34984
- KPMG Q3 2026 Global AI Pulse, 24 Sep 2026: 2,131 leaders, 20 countries, fieldwork 23 Jul-26 Aug. Planned investment avg $210m (from $186m Q1). Only 12% consistently assess AI value against cost (48% among those reporting returns). 86% of established ROI orgs operate formal "harness layer" vs 31% experimenting. 61% review AI costs at approval, 59% monitor in operation. 34% significant agent adoption (25% Q1). Heneghan quote. https://kpmg.com/xx/en/media/press-releases/2026/09/new-kpmg-ai-pulse-survey-as-ai-maturity-converges-leading-organizations-show-what-ai-at-scale-requires.html
- Gartner 28 May 2026: survey of 204 finance leaders, March 2026: 63% said 2025 AI implementation slower than expected. Steecker: "They must not mistake activity for impact." https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-28-gartner-says-cfos-must-stop-mistaking-finance-ai-deployment-for-value-creation
- Stanford AI Index 2026 (Apr 2026): corporate AI investment $581.7bn in 2025, +130%. Terminal-Bench agent success 20% -> 77.3%. Agents still lag on multi-step planning, financial analysis. https://hai.stanford.edu/news/inside-the-ai-index-12-takeaways-from-the-2026-report
