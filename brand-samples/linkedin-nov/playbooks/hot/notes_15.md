# Notes 15: EU AI Act timetable (verified 8 November 2026)

## Primary law
- AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, OJ L 12.7.2024. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj/eng
  - Art 113 (original): in force 20th day after publication (1 Aug 2024); applies 2 Aug 2026; Ch I and II (incl. Art 4 literacy, Art 5 prohibitions) from 2 Feb 2025; Ch III s4, Ch V (GPAI), Ch VII, Ch XII, Art 78 from 2 Aug 2025 except Art 101 (GPAI fines); Art 6(1) from 2 Aug 2027.
  - Art 111(3): GPAI models placed before 2 Aug 2025 comply by 2 Aug 2027.
  - Art 2(1)(a) providers placing on EU market irrespective of location; (c) third-country providers/deployers where output used in the Union.
  - Art 22: third-country providers of high-risk systems appoint EU authorised representative.
  - Art 25: deployer/distributor becomes provider if own name/trademark, substantial modification, or changes intended purpose to high-risk.
  - Art 26: deployer duties (use per instructions, human oversight with competent staff, input data, monitoring, incident reporting).
  - Art 99: fines up to EUR 35m/7% (Art 5); EUR 15m/3% (operators incl. Art 50 transparency, Art 26 deployers); EUR 7.5m/1% (misleading info). SMEs: lower of the two.
  - Original Art 4: "take measures to ensure, to their best extent, a sufficient level of AI literacy".
- Digital Omnibus on AI, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 of 8 July 2026, OJ L 24.7.2026; in force third day after publication (27 July 2026 per Commission page). https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1744/oj/eng
  - Art 113(c)(i): Ch III ss1-3 for Annex III / Art 6(2) from 2 Dec 2027; (c)(ii) Annex I / Art 6(1) from 2 Aug 2028.
  - Art 113(a): new Art 5(1)(ba),(bb), 5(1a),(1b) (non-consensual intimate imagery, CSAM generation) apply from 2 Dec 2026.
  - Art 111(4) new: providers of generative systems placed on market before 2 Aug 2026 comply with Art 50(2) by 2 Dec 2026.
  - Art 111(2): high-risk systems for public authorities by 2 Aug 2030; legacy high-risk systems only if significant design changes.
  - Art 4 replaced: "take measures to support the development of AI literacy"; "does not require ... guarantee any specific level"; Commission and Member States support.
  - New Art 4a: special category data for bias detection, strictly necessary, safeguards.
  - Art 57: national sandbox operational by 2 Aug 2027. SMCs (small mid-caps) get SME-style relief incl. penalty caps (Art 99(6a)).
  - Recital: delay because of "delayed availability of standards ... and delayed establishment of national competent authorities".
- European Parliament legislative resolution 16 June 2026, P10_TA(2026)0198, COM(2025)0836. https://data.europarl.europa.eu/distribution/doc/TA-10-2026-0198_en.pdf
- Council final adoption 29 June 2026 (Freshfields, 10 July 2026). https://www.freshfields.com/en/our-thinking/blogs/technology-quotient/eu-ai-act-unpacked-34-the-final-digital-omnibus-on-ai-key-amendments-to-the-a-102nber
  - Freshfields: other Art 50 duties still 2 Aug 2026; underlying high-risk obligations unchanged; register even if self-assessed non-high-risk (lighter).
