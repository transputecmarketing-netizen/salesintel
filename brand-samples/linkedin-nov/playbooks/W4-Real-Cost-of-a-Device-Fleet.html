Introduction

The invoice is only day one.

A device fleet is judged at purchase on price. It is paid for over its whole life: every set-up, support call, security update, repair and, finally, a safe and certified retirement.

Price is visible

The purchase cost is on one invoice, so it gets the scrutiny.

Running cost is scattered

Support, repairs and lost hours are spread across teams and budgets, so they rarely get added up.

Risk is the hidden cost

An unpatched, unencrypted or badly retired device can cost more than the fleet itself.

The lifecycle

Six stages, one fleet

Plan each stage before you buy, and the purchase decision changes.

Buy

Choose devices against real roles and refresh cycles, not just the unit price.

Deploy

Image, enrol, ship and get each person productive from their first day.

Support

Handle tickets, faults and warranty claims without people waiting on a fix.

Secure

Patch, encrypt, protect and know where every device is.

Retire

Wipe data to a certified standard, then recycle or resell responsibly.
Ask of every quote: what happens to this device after it arrives?

Before you buy

Questions to ask any supplier

Use these to compare offers on the whole lifecycle, not just the price per device.
About the devices
?How will these devices be set up and delivered to our people?
?What is the repair and replacement route, and how fast is it?
?How long will this model be supported with updates?
About the lifecycle
?How do you report on security and compliance across the fleet?
?What happens to devices at end of life, and do we get certificates?
?Can you recover value from devices we retire?
