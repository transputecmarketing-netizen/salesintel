# Crises Control capabilities: what you may say (round 3)

Verified against crises-control.com product pages, the use-case register and the operating framework (evidence table: cc/capabilities_evidence.md). Be conservative: if a claim is not on this page, do not make it.

## Standard (how each module works)
- **SOS**: press-and-hold alert in the mobile app for frontline or lone staff; raises an incident; shares the user's location where available (device and settings permitting). Say "location where available".
- **Ping**: alerts by push, SMS, voice, email and desktop/web; one-tap acknowledgement; delivery and acknowledgement tracking.
- **Incident Manager**: incident workspace; authorised users launch incidents and response plans; roles; a single communication log; message templates.
- **Task Manager**: tasks with owners and deadlines, assigned when a plan is activated; completion tracked.
- **Control Centre**: live view of status, who was notified and acknowledged, task progress and locations.
- **Audit & Reporting**: timestamped records of messages, acknowledgements, decisions logged and tasks; export as PDF or CSV.
- **CRAiG** (beta): answers natural-language questions and summarises the customer's own Crises Control data, within each user's permissions. It does not make decisions, take actions, launch or detect incidents. Never describe future features.

## Configured (the customer sets it up)
Say "where configured", "rules you set", "plans you build":
- response plans, task lists and templates per incident type
- groups, sites and audiences for targeting
- escalation rules and timings when people do not respond; cascading from one channel to the next; repeat notifications
- the response options people choose when they acknowledge (two-way responses)
- task order and escalation timings

## Integrations (third-party, where configured)
- directory sync: Microsoft Entra ID, SCIM, scheduled SFTP/CSV import
- single sign-on: Entra ID, Google Workspace, Okta
- Microsoft Teams app (enabled by an administrator)
- triggers from monitoring or other systems: only as "where an integration has been configured" (no product names)

## Do NOT claim
- that the platform detects incidents by itself, or triggers plans automatically without a configured integration
- Slack, ServiceNow or any other named integration not listed above; an API by name
- offline use of the app (the app needs an internet connection; SMS and voice reach people without data)
- geofencing or map-drawn targeting; conference bridges or dial-in calls
- "automatically escalates" without "under rules you configure"
- statistics, customers, response times or uptime figures for Crises Control
Exercises: you may say teams can run exercises and test alerts using the platform (the register lists simulation tests as a customer use), but do not describe a dedicated "test mode" feature.
