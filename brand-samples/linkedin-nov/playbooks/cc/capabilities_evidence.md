## Automatic detection / automatic triggering

- No current product page says Crises Control **detects** incidents. /solutions/it-alerting/ says detection comes from others: "An incident is detected by monitoring tools, integrations or team reports."
- Incidents are launched by people: "authorised users can activate an incident" (Incident Manager). The automation that does exist happens **after** that launch: the SOS alert creates an incident record, tasks are assigned when a plan is activated, and escalation and channel cascading follow configured rules.
- Triggering from external systems needs an API or monitoring integration plus configuration (FAQ REST API; UC l.35-36). The blog "How Automated IT Incident Response Works" (22 May 2026) describes webhook/syslog ingestion and correlation in general "platform" terms. It is not a product specification, so don't cite it as one.
- Suggested safe wording: "When an incident is reported or raised by an integrated system you have configured, Crises Control activates your predefined plan."

## CRAiG: exact current capability

- BETA. Included in the platform subscription, not a separate licence.
- Answers natural-language questions and summarises information from the customer's own Crises Control data: incident updates, plans, tasks, reports, procedures and communication records. Supports follow-up questions. Access follows existing user permissions and is logged for audit. A demo shows it generating an incident report and translating text. The FAQ claims 25+ languages.
- It does **not** make decisions, take response actions, launch incidents or detect incidents, and the page does not describe it recommending actions. Insight and recommendations are the future roadmap (FW l.1907-1925: Stage 2 Insight, Stage 3 Intelligence).

## Caveats

- The /integrations/ page lists only identity and provisioning integrations (Entra ID, Google Workspace, Okta, SCIM, SFTP/CSV). Teams has its own evidence on the app page. Slack, ServiceNow, Salesforce and the API appear only in the FAQ, older /products/ pages and blogs. Confirm them with Crises Control before naming them in playbooks.
- No public pricing page exists. The FAQ refers to modules added by quote ("obtain a quote for the module to be added"). The Teams app and the Windows desktop app are described as free.
