Week 1 · Mon 2 to Fri 6 Nov · AWS
01

AWS consultancy vs managed services

Which are you actually buying, a decision, or someone to run the estate? Getting it wrong is common and expensive.
Format
Editorial + optional quote card
Posting notes
Lead with the editorial. Use the quote card as a second post mid-week or as the first comment image.
Draft post copy
Consultancy or managed service? They sound alike. They are not the same purchase. Consultancy buys you a decision: the right architecture, the right migration path, a plan with an end date. A managed service buys you someone to run the estate: monitoring, patching and support, every day, against agreed service levels. Buying one when you needed the other is common, and expensive. Before your next AWS conversation, ask a simple question: do we need a decision, or do we need someone to run it?

03

What FinOps actually is

FinOps is not a tool, it is a discipline: visibility, accountability, optimisation and continuous review.
Format
36s vertical video (muted-safe) + myth vs reality card
Video file: w3-finops-explainer-1080x1920.mp4 · 36s · 30fps · muted-safe
Posting notes
Video reads fully with sound off. Add captions file only if a voiceover is added later. Myth vs reality card works as a follow-up post.
Draft post copy
FinOps is not a tool you buy. It is a discipline your teams practise. Four parts, one cycle: Visibility: see where every pound goes. Accountability: every cost has a named owner. Optimisation: spend with intent, not by default. Continuous review: check, adjust, repeat. Not a project. A habit.
#FinOps#AWS#CloudFinance#CloudGovernance

The real cost of a device fleet

Buying the hardware is the cheap part. The four years after it, running and securing it, are the cost.
Format
Editorial + carousel, 7 slides
Posting notes
CONFIRM FIRST: the procurement offer (supply-and-manage / lifecycle / resale). Slide 7 is written neutrally until confirmed. No Lenovo or Dell logos on the images; brand names can go in the caption if approved.
Draft post copy
The laptop is the cheap part. The invoice is only day one. After that, every device has to be deployed, supported, secured, repaired and, eventually, retired safely with its data wiped. That is where the real cost of a fleet lives, and it rarely shows up in the purchase decision. Plan the lifecycle, not just the purchase. [Offer line to confirm]

Your AWS cost-control checklist

The five-point plan to review your own cloud cost. The useful takeaway, given away free.
Format
Carousel, 8 slides
Posting notes
Designed to be saved and shared. Use as the bridge into February webinar promotion.
Draft post copy
A free checklist for your next monthly cloud review. 1. Give every resource an owner 2. Switch off what is idle 3. Clear out old storage 4. Match commitments to usage 5. Set alerts, then review Each check takes minutes in the AWS console. The value is in doing it every month. Save this, and share it with whoever owns your AWS bill. More on this in our February cost-control webinar.
